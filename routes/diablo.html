Diablo

Play the original Diablo in your browser with diabloweb - a WebAssembly port built on the source-released Devilution engine, embedded by hotlink to its official demo site. The shareware episode is included and starts instantly.

The shareware episode plays right away. For the full game you must supply your own DIABDAT.MPQ from a copy of Diablo you own.

Instructions

Controls

  • Move and attack with the mouse, or use the keyboard shortcuts from the in-game menu
  • Pick a class, then click to move and click a monster to attack; press Esc for the menu and open the character sheet to spend skill points

About

  • Diablo: the 1996 action RPG from Blizzard. The browser build is diabloweb (WebAssembly, based on the open-source Devilution engine), embedded by hotlink to its official demo page.
