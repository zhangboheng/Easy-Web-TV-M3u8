BBC Micro

BBC Micro Emulator (jsbeeb)

jsbeeb emulates the classic 1981 BBC Micro home computer (Model B), including its BASIC interpreter, cassette games and disc system.

Open Standalone

Powered by jsbeeb (GPL-3.0 by Matt Godbolt and contributors), embedded from the official bbc.godbolt.org deployment. Discs and cassettes load inside the emulator UI.

Instructions

Controls

  • Click inside the emulator screen, then type like on a real BBC Micro keyboard
  • Type CHAIN"GAMES" and press Enter at the BASIC prompt for the built-in game menu
