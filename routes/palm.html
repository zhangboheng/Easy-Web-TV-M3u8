Palm OS Games

Run Palm OS apps and games in your browser with CloudpilotEmu (GPL-3.0, embedded by hotlink to the official web app). It emulates Dragonball devices (Palm OS 1-4) and the ARM-based Tungsten E2 (OS 5).

No ROMs are bundled - a Palm OS ROM image is required and must be provided by you from your own device. Commercial software is not hot-linked here.

Instructions

Controls

  • Use the on-screen device buttons with mouse/touch, or the keyboard once the emulator has focus
  • Inside the emulator, pick a device and install a ROM image, then add your own .prc apps and databases

About

  • Palm OS: the classic PDA/palm-size platform (1996-2007) with a large library of games. Emulation via CloudpilotEmu (GPL-3.0), embedded by hotlink.
