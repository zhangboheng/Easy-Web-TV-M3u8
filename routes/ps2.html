PS2 Emulator

Run PlayStation 2 games in your browser with TabStationToo (PCSX2 compiled to WebAssembly, GPL-3.0, embedded by hotlink to the official web app). The Emotion Engine and GS are emulated with threads and hardware rendering via WebGPU.

No BIOS and no games are bundled - on first launch pick a BIOS dumped from your own console (.bin, usually 4 MB) and disc images of games you own (.iso); nothing is uploaded. The embedded player uses temporary isolated storage, so BIOS, memory cards and saves do not survive closing or reloading; use the “Open in a new tab” link for persistent local storage. Many games run at or near full speed.

Instructions

Controls

  • Games are controlled with keyboard or gamepad; keys are shown on-screen while the PS2 runs, and Escape releases the keyboard
  • On first launch open the Disc library to choose your BIOS and a folder with your disc images; BIOS, games, memory cards and save states persist in your browser

About

  • PS2: Sony's console (2000-2013). Emulation via TabStationToo (PCSX2, GPL-3.0), embedded by hotlink. Requires WebAssembly threads, SharedArrayBuffer and WebGPU - i.e. a recent desktop Chrome/Edge.
