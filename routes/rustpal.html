Legend of Sword and Fairy (DOS)

Play the classic 1995 DOS RPG in your browser with a complete Rust/WASM engine port running the original game data, embedded by hotlink to the official demo site. No DOSBox or local files are required.

The game data is hosted upstream by the project author. 《仙剑奇侠传》 is copyright of Softstar Entertainment; this page is for technical study and nostalgic research only, not commercial use. Sound starts after the first click or key press.

Instructions

Controls

  • Arrow keys move, Space/Enter confirms, Esc opens the menu. In battle: R repeat attack, A auto, D defend, E item, W throw, Q flee, F magic, S status
  • Touch devices show an on-screen D-pad and 确认/取消/连击 buttons. Click the canvas first so the iframe receives keyboard focus and audio starts.

About

  • Engine: GPL-3.0 Rust port from SDLPAL by madeye. Original game data © Softstar Entertainment; supplied by the upstream project for non-commercial study only. Requires modern browsers with WebAssembly and SharedArrayBuffer support.
