Zelda II: The Adventure of Link

Play Zelda II in your browser with z2rs - a clean-room reconstruction of the NES classic in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly and embedded by hotlink to its official web build. Bring your own ROM to start.

No game data is bundled - you must load your own Zelda II ROM. Only the No-Intro USA dump is accepted (CRC32 BA322865). The file is read in place and never uploaded. Not endorsed by Nintendo.

Instructions

Controls

  • Move with the arrow keys, jump and attack with the keyboard or a gamepad
  • Load your own Zelda II ROM on the start screen, then press Start; Tab fast-forwards, F5/F7 save and load states, F11 toggles fullscreen

About

  • Zelda II: The Adventure of Link (1987, Nintendo). z2rs is a clean-room reconstruction in Rust, checked frame by frame against the original ROM, adding widescreen, save states and optional online co-op. Source is MIT licensed; the game itself is not included.
